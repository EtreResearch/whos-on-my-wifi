# Who's on My Wi-Fi

Anyone on a Wi-Fi network wants to know how many devices share it, and what they are called.

## Language

**Device**: A phone, computer, or other piece of equipment on the network. A device is not proof of who is using it.

**Responding device**: A device that answered this scan, plus the Mac running the app. This is an observed minimum, not a complete router connection count.

**Device name**: The name a device announces about itself on the network, such as "Rahuls-iPhone". Chosen by the device's owner, often hidden, and never proof of who the person is. A device without one is an **Unknown device**.

**Partial scan**: A scan of only the addresses nearest this computer on a network too large to check fully. Its count covers that part alone, so the network's real total is likely higher.

**Hidden network**: A network that stops devices seeing each other (often called client isolation), common on public Wi-Fi. Only this computer is visible, so no device count is possible there.

**Person**: Someone using the network. One person may use several devices, and a device says nothing reliable about who they are.
